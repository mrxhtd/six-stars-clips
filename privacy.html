Six Stars Clips poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What we access

When an account owner connects TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the display name and account ID) from that platform. It uses them only to upload videos to that same account and to check the upload's status.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable by that user alone. They are never sold, shared or sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.

What we don't do

We don't collect data about viewers, followers or anyone else, don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and don't share data with third parties.

Deleting data

Disconnecting the app in your platform account settings revokes its access. Deleting the token files removes everything the tool stored.
