Six Stars Clips poster: Terms of Service

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What it is

The Six Stars Clips poster is a private tool run by the owner of the Six Stars Clips accounts. It uploads short videos we edited to our own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Use of platforms

The tool only acts on accounts that their owner has connected by signing in with the platform's official login. It follows each platform's terms, community guidelines and API rules. You can disconnect it at any time from your account's settings on each platform.

Content

Clips credit the original creator and link to the source. If you are a creator and want a clip removed, contact us and we will take it down.
